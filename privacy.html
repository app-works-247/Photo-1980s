1. Overview & Developer Identity

Welcome to Rad Lab ("the App", "Application"), developed and operated by AppWorks 247 ("we", "us", or "our"). We are dedicated to providing an enjoyable vintage photo transformation experience while maintaining the utmost respect for your privacy and personal data.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Rad Lab mobile application on Apple iOS and our official project website hosted at https://app-works-247.github.io/Photo-1980s/. It describes how we collect, use, process, and safeguard your data, in full compliance with Apple's App Store Review Guidelines (Guidelines 5.1.1 and 5.1.2), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and Vietnam's Personal Data Protection Decree (Decree 13/2023/ND-CP).

2. Information We Collect

We only collect the data needed to run the app. Most of Rad Lab works on your device and collects nothing:

  • On-Device Studio: The Retro Camera, Darkroom, backdrops, prints, layouts, caption suggestions and the Develop Photo shortcut work on your device. Backdrop cut-outs use Apple's Vision framework and caption suggestions use Apple's on-device model where available. Photos used in these features are not sent to us.
  • User Account Data: When you sign in using Apple ID ("Sign in with Apple") or email, we receive your email address or an anonymous relay Apple ID, along with an encrypted authentication token generated by Supabase Auth.
  • Photos You Send to the AI Photo Lab: When you use an AI Photo Lab tool (portrait styles, AI Yearbook, Restore & Colorize or VHS Home Video) and agree to send a photo, the photo you chose is uploaded over TLS to our private cloud storage so the result can be made.
  • App Preferences: Your language and settings are stored on your device. The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports.

📌 We DO NOT collect: Contacts, browsing history, location data, microphone audio, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or social graph data.

3. How We Process Photos & AI Models

Rad Lab's studio runs on your device. Only the optional AI Photo Lab sends a photo off your device, and only like this:

  1. After you agree in the app, the photo you chose is sent over an encrypted HTTPS/TLS connection to our cloud backend.
  2. Our server sends it to an AI provider (OpenAI or fal.ai) only to make the result you asked for: an 80s portrait, a yearbook portrait, a restored and colorized photo, or a few seconds of video for a VHS clip.
  3. The generated result is saved to your private generation history accessible only through your authenticated account.

🛡️ ZERO MODEL TRAINING: We do NOT use your personal photos to train, retrain, or improve any public foundation AI models without your explicit prior consent.

4. Face Data Policy: Collection, Use, Sharing, Storage, and Retention

Rad Lab is committed to safeguarding user privacy and handling face data with the highest standards of security, transparency, and compliance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines 2.1, 5.1.1(i), and 5.1.2(i).

4.1 What Face Data We Collect

  • User-Selected Photos Only: The App uploads only the photos you choose for an AI Photo Lab tool. Photos used only in the on-device studio are never uploaded. To place a backdrop or check a photo before an AI request, the App finds people and faces on your device with Apple's Vision framework; nothing from that check is stored or sent.
  • No Biometric Identification or Face Templates: The App does NOT extract, calculate, or store unique biometric identifiers, facial vector geometry, facial mesh landmarks, or iris templates for individual identification, surveillance, or authentication. Facial features in the photo are treated strictly as visual pixels for artistic style transformation.

4.2 Purpose and Planned Use of Face Data

  • AI Photo Lab Results Only: Photos that contain faces are processed only to make the result you asked for: an 80s portrait style, an AI Yearbook portrait, a restored and colorized photo, or a VHS clip.
  • Zero AI Model Training Guarantee: We NEVER use your personal photos, portraits, or face data to train, retrain, fine-tune, or improve any public or private artificial intelligence foundation models. Your data remains strictly your own.
  • No Advertising or Profiling: Face data is never used for advertising, marketing, user tracking across apps, or commercial profiling.

4.3 Third-Party Sharing & Equal Protection Confirmation (Guidelines 5.1.1(i) & 5.1.2(i))

When you use an AI Photo Lab tool, the photo you chose, which may contain faces, is sent over TLS to these providers:

  • OpenAI, Inc. — Provides the GPT-Image API used for some AI Photo Lab image edits.
  • fal.ai (Features & Labels Inc.) — Provides image-editing and image-to-video models used for portrait styles, the AI Yearbook, restoration and VHS clips.
  • Supabase, Inc. — Provides private cloud infrastructure and secure encrypted object storage.

🛡️ CONFIRMATION OF EQUAL PROTECTION: In strict compliance with Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.2(i), we confirm that all third-party AI service providers with whom face data is shared provide the same or equal protection of user data as set forth in this Privacy Policy. Under their API terms, these providers do not use data sent through their APIs to train their models, do not sell it, and keep it only for a limited time for security and abuse monitoring.

In-App Explicit Consent (Guideline 5.1.1(i)): The App presents an interactive AI Data Processing & Sharing consent dialog and obtains your explicit affirmative permission before any photo or face data is transmitted to third-party AI providers. You can review or revoke this permission at any time in Profile > AI & Third-Party Disclosure.

4.4 Storage Practices & Locations

  • Private Encrypted Cloud Storage: Photos sent to the AI Photo Lab and their results (images and VHS clips) are stored in private storage buckets managed by Supabase, on Amazon Web Services (AWS) infrastructure in Sydney, Australia (ap-southeast-2).
  • Row-Level Security & Encryption: All files are encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS. Each user's images are strictly isolated under Postgres Row-Level Security (RLS) policies matching the authenticated user ID; no other user can access your photos.
  • Third-Party Processing: OpenAI and fal.ai process the photo to make the result and return it to our server. Under their own policies they may keep request data for a limited time for security and abuse monitoring, and they do not use it to train their models.

4.5 Retention Period & Deletion Practices

  • Retention Period: User photos and generated portraits are retained in your private cloud gallery only for as long as your authenticated account remains active or until you manually delete them. AI providers may keep request data only for a limited time for security and abuse monitoring, as described in 4.4.
  • Individual Photo Deletion (In-App): You can permanently delete any AI result at any time in the in-app Gallery by opening it and tapping the Delete (trash) icon. The file is immediately removed from our cloud storage. VHS clips are removed when you delete your account, or on request by email.
  • Permanent Account & Data Erasure (In-App): You can permanently delete your entire account, all profile information, credit history, and all stored face photos at any time by navigating to Profile > Delete Account within the app. Upon confirmation, all your data is instantly and permanently deleted from our active databases and cloud storage.
  • Manual Deletion Request: You can also request immediate manual data deletion by contacting us directly at appworks.247@gmail.com.

5. Third-Party Service Providers & Equal Protection

To deliver reliable infrastructure, Rad Lab integrates select enterprise service providers, each providing equal or greater data protection:

  • Supabase, Inc. (PostgreSQL, Auth & Storage): Manages user authentication, credit balances, and private file storage with Row-Level Security (RLS) on AWS in Sydney, Australia.
  • Apple Inc. (StoreKit & Sign In with Apple): Handles all payment transactions, subscriptions, and private identity authentication on iOS.
  • OpenAI, Inc. (GPT-Image API): Processes some AI Photo Lab image edits over encrypted TLS connections. Does not use API data to train its models; may keep it for a limited time for abuse monitoring.
  • fal.ai / Features & Labels Inc. (AI Image and Video Models): Runs the image-editing and image-to-video models for portrait styles, the AI Yearbook, restoration and VHS clips. Does not use the data to train models or share it with others.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data or photos to data brokers or third-party advertisers.

6. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

You maintain total control over your photos and your account at all times:

  • Deleting Individual Photos: You can open any generation in your in-app Gallery, tap the Delete icon, and that photo is immediately and permanently removed from our cloud storage.
  • Permanent Account Deletion (In-App): You can navigate to Profile > Delete Account within the app. Confirming deletion immediately wipes your profile, credit balance, transaction history, and all stored photos from our active database and storage systems.

7. In-App Purchases & Financial Data

All purchases, the Pro subscription (and the earlier Basic and Studio plans for existing subscribers) and consumable credit packs, are processed by Apple's App Store through StoreKit.

AppWorks 247 does NOT collect, view, or store your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or banking information. Your financial data is protected under Apple's secure payment infrastructure.

8. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Vietnam PDPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You may request a copy of your personal data.
  • Right to Rectification: You may correct inaccurate information in your profile.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request complete erasure of your data via the in-app Delete Account feature or email.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You can stop using the service and delete your account at any time.

9. Children's Privacy

Rad Lab is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information or photographs from children under these ages. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will immediately delete it from our servers.
