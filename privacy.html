1. Overview & Developer Identity

Welcome to PhotoAI 1980s ("the App", "Application"), developed and operated by AppWorks 247 ("we", "us", or "our"). We are dedicated to providing an enjoyable vintage photo transformation experience while maintaining the utmost respect for your privacy and personal data.

This Privacy Policy applies to the PhotoAI 1980s mobile application on Apple iOS and our official project website hosted at https://app-works-247.github.io/Photo-1980s/. It describes how we collect, use, process, and safeguard your data, in full compliance with Apple's App Store Review Guidelines (Guidelines 5.1.1 and 5.1.2), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and Vietnam's Personal Data Protection Decree (Decree 13/2023/ND-CP).

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data strictly necessary to provide the core AI portrait generation services:

  • User Account Data: When you sign in using Apple ID ("Sign in with Apple") or email, we receive your email address or an anonymous relay Apple ID, along with an encrypted authentication token generated by Supabase Auth.
  • User-Uploaded Photos: When you select or capture a photo to generate an 80s portrait, that image file is uploaded via TLS encryption to our private cloud storage for the sole purpose of AI image generation.
  • App Preferences & Technical Diagnostics: Language preference (English/Vietnamese), app version number, device model, and non-identifying crash logs to ensure app stability.

📌 We DO NOT collect: Contacts, browsing history, location data, microphone audio, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or social graph data.

3. How We Process Photos & AI Models

The core functionality of PhotoAI 1980s is transforming user portrait photos into vintage 1980s aesthetic styles:

  1. Your uploaded photo is sent over an encrypted HTTPS/TLS connection directly to our secure cloud backend.
  2. The image is processed by dedicated image editing models (e.g., Fal.ai, GPT-Image) strictly to apply 1980s hairstyle, retro clothing, lighting, and film grain while maintaining facial resemblance.
  3. The generated result is saved to your private generation history accessible only through your authenticated account.

🛡️ ZERO MODEL TRAINING: We do NOT use your personal photos to train, retrain, or improve any public foundation AI models without your explicit prior consent.

4. Biometric & Facial Data Disclaimer

We believe in complete transparency regarding facial analysis:

  • No Biometric Identification: PhotoAI 1980s does NOT extract, calculate, or store unique biometric identifiers (such as facial vector geometry or iris templates) used for individual identification or surveillance.
  • Temporary Visual Feature Mapping: The AI visual processing analyzes pixels solely during the temporary generation request to blend 80s artistic elements onto the photo.
  • No Face Database: We do not maintain any face database, facial recognition registry, or cross-platform matching system.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

To deliver reliable infrastructure, PhotoAI 1980s integrates select enterprise service providers:

  • Supabase (PostgreSQL, Auth & Storage): Manages secure user authentication, credit balances, and isolated file storage with Row-Level Security (RLS).
  • Apple Inc. (StoreKit & Sign In with Apple): Handles all payment transactions, subscriptions, and private identity authentication on iOS.
  • AI Inference Partners (Fal.ai, OpenAI): Encrypted stateless API calls solely for generating requested retro portraits during the active transaction.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data or photos to data brokers or third-party advertisers.

6. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

You maintain total control over your photos and your account at all times:

  • Deleting Individual Photos: You can open any generation in your in-app Gallery, tap the Delete icon, and that photo is immediately and permanently removed from our cloud storage.
  • Permanent Account Deletion (In-App): You can navigate to Profile > Delete Account within the app. Confirming deletion immediately wipes your profile, credit balance, transaction history, and all stored photos from our active database and storage systems.

7. In-App Purchases & Financial Data

All purchases of subscriptions (Basic, Pro, Studio) and consumable Credit Packs (Starter, Value, Power) are processed directly through Apple's App Store via the StoreKit framework.

AppWorks 247 does NOT collect, view, or store your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or banking information. Your financial data is protected under Apple's secure payment infrastructure.

8. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Vietnam PDPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You may request a copy of your personal data.
  • Right to Rectification: You may correct inaccurate information in your profile.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request complete erasure of your data via the in-app Delete Account feature or email.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You can stop using the service and delete your account at any time.

9. Children's Privacy

PhotoAI 1980s is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information or photographs from children under these ages. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will immediately delete it from our servers.
