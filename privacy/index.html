1. Overview & Developer Identity

Welcome to PhotoAI 1980s ("the App", "Application"), developed and operated by AppWorks 247 ("we", "us", or "our"). We are dedicated to providing an enjoyable vintage photo transformation experience while maintaining the utmost respect for your privacy and personal data.

This Privacy Policy applies to the PhotoAI 1980s mobile application on Apple iOS and our official project website hosted at https://app-works-247.github.io/Photo-1980s/. It describes how we collect, use, process, and safeguard your data, in full compliance with Apple's App Store Review Guidelines (Guidelines 5.1.1 and 5.1.2), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and Vietnam's Personal Data Protection Decree (Decree 13/2023/ND-CP).

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data strictly necessary to provide the core AI portrait generation services:

  • User Account Data: When you sign in using Apple ID ("Sign in with Apple") or email, we receive your email address or an anonymous relay Apple ID, along with an encrypted authentication token generated by Supabase Auth.
  • User-Uploaded Photos: When you select or capture a photo to generate an 80s portrait, that image file is uploaded via TLS encryption to our private cloud storage for the sole purpose of AI image generation.
  • App Preferences & Technical Diagnostics: Language preference (English/Vietnamese), app version number, device model, and non-identifying crash logs to ensure app stability.

📌 We DO NOT collect: Contacts, browsing history, location data, microphone audio, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or social graph data.

3. How We Process Photos & AI Models

The core functionality of PhotoAI 1980s is transforming user portrait photos into vintage 1980s aesthetic styles:

  1. Your uploaded photo is sent over an encrypted HTTPS/TLS connection directly to our secure cloud backend.
  2. The image is processed by dedicated image editing models (e.g., Fal.ai, GPT-Image) strictly to apply 1980s hairstyle, retro clothing, lighting, and film grain while maintaining facial resemblance.
  3. The generated result is saved to your private generation history accessible only through your authenticated account.

🛡️ ZERO MODEL TRAINING: We do NOT use your personal photos to train, retrain, or improve any public foundation AI models without your explicit prior consent.

4. Face Data Policy: Collection, Use, Sharing, Storage, and Retention

PhotoAI 1980s is committed to safeguarding user privacy and handling face data with the highest standards of security, transparency, and compliance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines 2.1, 5.1.1(i), and 5.1.2(i).

4.1 What Face Data We Collect

  • User-Selected Photos Only: The App collects only digital portrait photos containing human faces that you explicitly and voluntarily choose from your photo library or capture using the device's camera.
  • No Biometric Identification or Face Templates: The App does NOT extract, calculate, or store unique biometric identifiers, facial vector geometry, facial mesh landmarks, or iris templates for individual identification, surveillance, or authentication. Facial features in the photo are treated strictly as visual pixels for artistic style transformation.

4.2 Purpose and Planned Use of Face Data

  • Vintage Portrait Transformation: Face data is collected and processed for the sole and exclusive purpose of styling your photo into your selected 1980s aesthetic portrait (applying retro hairstyles, vintage wardrobe, neon studio lighting, and film grain while maintaining facial resemblance).
  • Zero AI Model Training Guarantee: We NEVER use your personal photos, portraits, or face data to train, retrain, fine-tune, or improve any public or private artificial intelligence foundation models. Your data remains strictly your own.
  • No Advertising or Profiling: Face data is never used for advertising, marketing, user tracking across apps, or commercial profiling.

4.3 Third-Party Sharing & Equal Protection Confirmation (Guidelines 5.1.1(i) & 5.1.2(i))

To execute high-quality 1980s vintage portrait generation, your uploaded photo containing face data is securely transmitted via TLS encryption to our trusted third-party AI inference providers:

  • OpenAI, Inc. — Provides the GPT-Image API for vintage portrait editing and composite synthesis.
  • Fal.ai (Features Analytics Inc.) — Provides specialized generative diffusion endpoints for 80s aesthetic rendering.
  • Supabase, Inc. — Provides private cloud infrastructure and secure encrypted object storage.

🛡️ CONFIRMATION OF EQUAL PROTECTION: In strict compliance with Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.2(i), we confirm that all third-party AI service providers with whom face data is shared provide the same or equal protection of user data as set forth in this Privacy Policy. Specifically, our contractual agreements and partner API policies strictly prohibit third-party AI partners from retaining user face data beyond transient processing, prohibit them from using user photos to train AI models, and prohibit them from selling or disclosing user photos.

In-App Explicit Consent (Guideline 5.1.1(i)): The App presents an interactive AI Data Processing & Sharing consent dialog and obtains your explicit affirmative permission before any photo or face data is transmitted to third-party AI providers. You can review or revoke this permission at any time in Profile > AI & Third-Party Disclosure.

4.4 Storage Practices & Locations

  • Private Encrypted Cloud Storage: Uploaded input photos and generated vintage portraits are stored in isolated private storage buckets managed by Supabase, hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS) infrastructure located in the US-East region.
  • Row-Level Security & Encryption: All files are encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.3. Each user's images are strictly isolated under Postgres Row-Level Security (RLS) policies matching the authenticated user ID; no other user can access your photos.
  • Stateless Third-Party Processing: Our third-party AI inference partners (OpenAI and Fal.ai) process images statelessly in temporary volatile memory and do not persistently store user photos or face data on their servers.

4.5 Retention Period & Deletion Practices

  • Retention Period: User photos and generated portraits are retained in your private cloud gallery only for as long as your authenticated account remains active or until you manually delete them. Third-party AI inference partners maintain zero persistent storage of face data; temporary operational processing logs are retained for at most 30 days strictly for security monitoring and abuse prevention, after which they are permanently deleted.
  • Individual Photo Deletion (In-App): You can permanently delete any photo at any time directly in the in-app Gallery by tapping the photo and clicking the Delete (trash) icon. The image file is immediately and irreversibly purged from our cloud storage.
  • Permanent Account & Data Erasure (In-App): You can permanently delete your entire account, all profile information, credit history, and all stored face photos at any time by navigating to Profile > Delete Account within the app. Upon confirmation, all your data is instantly and permanently deleted from our active databases and cloud storage.
  • Manual Deletion Request: You can also request immediate manual data deletion by contacting us directly at appworks.247@gmail.com.

5. Third-Party Service Providers & Equal Protection

To deliver reliable infrastructure, PhotoAI 1980s integrates select enterprise service providers, each providing equal or greater data protection:

  • Supabase, Inc. (PostgreSQL, Auth & Storage): Manages secure user authentication, credit balances, and isolated file storage with Row-Level Security (RLS) on AWS US-East.
  • Apple Inc. (StoreKit & Sign In with Apple): Handles all payment transactions, subscriptions, and private identity authentication on iOS.
  • OpenAI, Inc. (GPT-Image API): Processes portrait styling requests over encrypted TLS connections. Strictly prohibited from using user photos to train models or retaining data beyond transient processing.
  • Fal.ai / Features Analytics Inc. (Diffusion AI Inference): Executes specialized generative portrait transformations statelessly without persistent storage, model training, or third-party sharing.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data or photos to data brokers or third-party advertisers.

6. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

You maintain total control over your photos and your account at all times:

  • Deleting Individual Photos: You can open any generation in your in-app Gallery, tap the Delete icon, and that photo is immediately and permanently removed from our cloud storage.
  • Permanent Account Deletion (In-App): You can navigate to Profile > Delete Account within the app. Confirming deletion immediately wipes your profile, credit balance, transaction history, and all stored photos from our active database and storage systems.

7. In-App Purchases & Financial Data

All purchases of subscriptions (Basic, Pro, Studio) and consumable Credit Packs (Starter, Value, Power) are processed directly through Apple's App Store via the StoreKit framework.

AppWorks 247 does NOT collect, view, or store your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or banking information. Your financial data is protected under Apple's secure payment infrastructure.

8. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Vietnam PDPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You may request a copy of your personal data.
  • Right to Rectification: You may correct inaccurate information in your profile.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request complete erasure of your data via the in-app Delete Account feature or email.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You can stop using the service and delete your account at any time.

9. Children's Privacy

PhotoAI 1980s is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information or photographs from children under these ages. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will immediately delete it from our servers.
